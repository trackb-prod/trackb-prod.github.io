Use public resources responsibly.

This publishing framework sets basic expectations without pretending to be a finalized service contract.

Visitors should not misuse public forms, repositories, support channels, or website resources to distribute malware, probe systems without authorization, impersonate others, harass people, expose credentials, or interfere with normal service.

Support and repository use

  • Do not post passwords, access tokens, private keys, payment information, or sensitive personal data.
  • Use security channels for sensitive vulnerability information instead of public issues.
  • Keep support requests relevant, reproducible, and respectful.
  • Do not automate abusive traffic or attempt to bypass access controls.

This page is a general website framework. Final contractual acceptable-use terms should be reviewed against the actual services and applicable law.
